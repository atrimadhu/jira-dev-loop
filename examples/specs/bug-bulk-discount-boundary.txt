Bug: an order of exactly $100.00 does not get the 10% bulk discount

Where: cart.py (Cart.discount)

What we promise customers:
  "Spend $100 or more and get 10% off your order."

Steps to reproduce:
  1. Create an empty Cart.
  2. Add one item: sku "A1", name "Desk Lamp", unit_price 50.00, qty 2.
  3. Call cart.total().

Expected: 90.00   (subtotal 100.00, minus 10% = 90.00)
Actual:   100.00  (no discount applied)

Orders above $100 (e.g. 100.01) get the discount correctly. Orders below
$100 correctly get no discount. It's only the exact-threshold case that's
wrong, which looks like a > vs >= comparison.

Impact: customers who build a cart to exactly $100 to hit the promo are
charged full price. Support has had several complaints.
